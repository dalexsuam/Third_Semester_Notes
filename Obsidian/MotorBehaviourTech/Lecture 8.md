How we can measure forces with the strain gauge? We can use a method which looks at the geometry of the resistive wire that can be analyzed as a wire of lenght L, and a tranversal area A of the wire, and then, we have a cantilever beam which represents a deforming status which presents 4 glued strain gauges, this gauges have a resistance. This deformation would depend on where we're loading in the load cell, it can be compressed or extended, this compression and extension are used for making the measurement, starting from the formula of the Resistance depeding of its resistivity, lenght and area
